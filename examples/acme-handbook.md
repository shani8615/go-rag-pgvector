# Acme Cloud — Customer Handbook

## Refunds

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Support hours

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC.
Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support with a 1-hour response time.

## Data retention

Backups are kept for 35 days. Deleted projects can be restored for 14 days,
after which they are permanently removed.
